# Comparison uses Core Vehicle Identity, year, and mileage

Car Flip Search defines the Core Vehicle Identity as make plus Model Variant.
An Auto Trader Listing becomes a Market Comparable when it has the same Core
Vehicle Identity and registration year as the Candidate Vehicle and falls
within the Mileage Band. Fuel type, transmission, body style, door count, and
Trim Match remain informational evidence and may be missing or different.

This follows the buyer's comparison workflow: two listings for the same make
and model remain useful even when one seller omits a detail, while registration
year and materially different mileage constrain the fairness of the comparison.
Source acquisition therefore retains records with missing Vehicle Details
instead of excluding them from market evidence.
